About Me

I'm a postdoctoral researcher in particle physics at the University of Münster, with research spanning fundamental interactions and AI-driven modeling for large-scale physics experiments.

Within the ErUM-Wave project, I develop transformer-based architectures for 3D seismic wavefield prediction to advance Newtonian-noise mitigation for the Einstein Telescope, the next-generation gravitational-wave detector. My work integrates explainable sequence-model learning with high-performance computing for next-generation detectors. I also contribute to ML analyses within the IceCube Collaboration in the context of multi-messenger astrophysics.

I write tutorials and technical articles that bridge research papers and working code, on scientific ML, probabilistic forecasting, transformer models for physics, and AI methods for the physical sciences. Read the blog →
